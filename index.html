+PLUS add-on catalog

The signed list of add-ons that +PLUS offers on the phone. This is a staging catalog for testing.

Catalog link (+PLUS has it built in; it can also be entered when importing the key):
https://plus-for-grapheneos.github.io/catalog/index.json#sha256=8fed9c0c25b05e8c3c35faf7ff60e0d2b6ae80d65a1166af494598b36bdb50a7

Files: index.json (add-ons) and models.json (on-device AI models), each with a .sig signature. A phone accepts a catalog only if its key matches the hash in the link, its signature checks, it has not expired, and its serial is not older than the last one seen.
